Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

At Barq Al-Contact For Information Technology, we strive to ensure every transaction is processed smoothly and accurately. This Refund Policy outlines the conditions under which refunds may be issued for recharge and bill payment transactions made through our platform.

2. Eligible Refund Scenarios

Refunds may be issued in the following circumstances:

  • Failed transaction: Payment was deducted but the recharge or bill payment was not processed
  • Duplicate charge: The same transaction was charged more than once
  • System error: A technical malfunction caused an incorrect amount to be charged
  • Service unavailability: The service was unavailable at the time of the transaction and the amount was deducted

3. Non-Refundable Transactions

Refunds will not be issued in the following cases:

  • Successfully completed recharges delivered to the correct phone number
  • Recharges sent to an incorrect number due to user input error
  • Successfully processed bill payments
  • Transactions where the user has already consumed the recharged balance
  • Requests submitted more than 30 days after the transaction date

4. Incorrect Number Recharges

If you accidentally recharge the wrong phone number, please contact our customer support team immediately. While we cannot guarantee a refund in such cases, we will make reasonable efforts to assist you. The sooner you report the error, the higher the likelihood of a resolution.

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please follow these steps:

  • Contact our customer support team through the "Support" section
  • Provide your transaction reference number
  • Include the date and time of the transaction
  • Describe the reason for the refund request
  • Provide proof of payment (e.g., bank statement or screenshot)

6. Processing Time

Refund requests are reviewed within 3-5 business days from the date of submission. If approved, refunds are processed as follows:

  • Credit/Debit card: 5-14 business days to appear on your statement
  • Bank transfer: 5-10 business days
  • Electronic wallet: 1-5 business days

Please note that processing times may vary depending on your bank or payment provider.

7. Partial Refunds

In some cases, partial refunds may be issued if only a portion of the transaction was affected by an error or if applicable fees need to be deducted. You will be informed of the refund amount before it is processed.

8. Dispute Resolution

If you disagree with a refund decision, you may request a review by contacting our support team with additional documentation or evidence. All disputes are reviewed on a case-by-case basis, and our decision after the review is final.
